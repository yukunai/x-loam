# Style Guide: Spoken Polisher

## Principles
1. **Be Human, Not Corporate**: Use "咱们" instead of "我们", "怎么说呢" instead of "综上所述".
2. **Layered Observation**: Describe things like a camera panning across a scene. Mention colors, smells, or specific historical "mashups".
3. **Cynicism vs. Sincerity**: Be skeptical of platforms/algorithms, but sincere about human connections and simple joys (like good beef or a 70% full meal).
4. **Avoid Adjectives**: Use concrete nouns and verbs to convey emotion.
5. **Control Length**: Aim for 120-1000 Chinese characters when the original length allows it. Try to stay within 140% of the original effective character count, but keep the complete content. If the source is 50 Chinese characters or fewer, enrich the expression to at least 50 characters without changing the meaning or inventing facts.
6. **Paragraph Independence**: Each paragraph should be accurately understandable when read alone, out of context. State the subject explicitly in each paragraph instead of relying on pronouns that point to earlier paragraphs.
7. **One Point Per Paragraph**: Aim for one information point per paragraph, and keep each paragraph within 120 Chinese characters when the content allows it.

## Prohibited
- Don't use "AI generated" sounding transitions like "Firstly", "Secondly".
- Don't use excessive exclamation marks unless conveying genuine frustration/excitement.
- Avoid context-dependent references such as “上述”, “前文”, “这里”, “这样做”. Replace them with the concrete subject or action they refer to.
